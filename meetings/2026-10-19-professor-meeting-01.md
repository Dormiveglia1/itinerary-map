## 2. Discussion Summary

### 2.1 Project Complexity

The professor reviewed our SRS and raised concerns about whether the project would demonstrate sufficient algorithmic complexity for a capstone project.

The main concern was that the SRS did not clearly specify which optimization algorithm(s) would be implemented.

The professor emphasized the importance of developing a sufficiently complex optimization approach rather than relying on a basic routing algorithm.

### 2.2 Application Workflow

The team provided an overview of the proposed application:

1. Users select a destination city.
2. Users enter their preferences and constraints, including points of interest (POIs).
3. The system processes these inputs using an optimization algorithm.
4. The application generates an optimized itinerary that attempts to satisfy the specified constraints.

The team clarified that itinerary generation would be algorithm-based rather than relying on a Large Language Model (LLM).

### 2.3 PoC and Final Project Scope

The team explained the difference between the planned PoC and the final submission:

- **PoC:** Generate a one-day itinerary.
- **Final Submission:** Support itinerary generation for 1–3 days.

### 2.4 Optimization Algorithm

The professor asked what optimization algorithm the team planned to implement.

At the time of the meeting, the team had not yet selected a specific algorithm.

The professor expressed concern that a basic algorithm might not provide sufficient technical complexity and requested a clearer explanation of the intended optimization approach.
